## 🔬 Analyst notes: hand ranking and verdict

_Written after reading the abstracts, and the full text where available, of this category's papers. The hand ranking weighs technical merit and usefulness for a runtime or model builder, not just citations. The automatic impact ranking follows below._

**Verdict.** Distillation is the largest compression bucket (~250 papers), and in 2026 it is dominated by one idea:
**on-policy distillation (OPD)**. The student samples its own trajectories and the teacher gives **dense per-token
supervision** (reverse KL on student-visited states). This removes the exposure bias of SFT and sequence-level KD, gives
RL-like generalization at SFT-like cost, and is now used in frontier post-training (Qwen3 strong-to-weak, MiMo-V2-Flash's
multi-teacher OPD). The main threads:

1. **Making OPD work reliably.**
   * [Rethinking OPD](2604.13016-rethinking-on-policy-distillation-of-large-language-models-phenomenolo.md): success = progressive alignment on a small shared high-probability token set (97–99%
     of mass). Fix failures with an off-policy cold start + teacher-aligned prompts.
   * [Revisiting OPD](2603.25562-revisiting-on-policy-distillation-empirical-failure-modes-and-simple-f.md): sampled-token OPD has three failure modes; distilling over the teacher-supported
     token set gives +19.8%.
   * [Entropy-aware OPD](2603.07079-entropy-aware-on-policy-distillation-of-language-models.md) (ICML'26): add forward KL where the teacher is uncertain.
   * [Lightning OPD](2604.13010-lightning-opd-efficient-post-training-for-large-reasoning-models-with.md): *offline* OPD with teacher consistency; Qwen3-8B to 69.9% AIME'24 in **30 GPU-hours**,
     no live teacher server.
   * [OPD II](2609.04172-rethinking-on-policy-distillation-of-large-language-models-ii-one-trai.md): a single prompt already covers 71.5% of the states full-data OPD visits.
2. **Multi-teacher and cross-scale.**
   * [MOPD](2606.30406-mopd-multi-teacher-on-policy-distillation-for-capability-integration-i.md): domain teachers developed independently, merged into one student on its own rollouts. Beats
     mixed RL, cascaded RL and parameter merging on Qwen3-30B-A3B.
   * [Direct-OPD](2607.05394-weak-to-strong-generalization-via-direct-on-policy-distillation.md): transfer the teacher's *RL-induced shift* (log-ratio to its pre-RL reference) → weak
     teachers improve strong students (Qwen3-1.7B 48.3 → 58.3 AIME'24 in 4 h on 8×A100).
   * [GAD](2511.10643-black-box-on-policy-distillation-of-large-language-models.md): black-box OPD with a discriminator; Qwen2.5-14B ≈ GPT-5-Chat on LMSYS-Chat.
3. **Self-distillation** (teacher = the same model with privileged context: the answer, a demonstration, a
   self-revision):
   * [SDFT](2601.19897-self-distillation-enables-continual-learning.md): continual learning without forgetting;
   * [OPSD](2601.18734-self-distilled-reasoner-on-policy-self-distillation-for-large-language.md), [SD-Zero](2604.12002-self-distillation-zero-self-revision-turns-binary-rewards-into-dense-s.md), [RLSD](2604.03128-self-distilled-rlvr.md).

   It has sharp failure modes: it suppresses uncertainty expression, with up to −40% OOD
   ([why self-distillation degrades reasoning](2603.24472-why-does-self-distillation-sometimes-degrade-the-reasoning-capability.md)), and privileged teachers leak.
4. **Classic logit KD at scale, for pretraining and pruning recovery.**
   * [Distillation Scaling Laws](2502.08606-distillation-scaling-laws.md) (Apple, ICML'25): distillation beats supervised pretraining **only if the
     teacher already exists or serves many students**, up to a predictable compute level.
   * [Distilled pretraining](2509.01649-distilled-pretraining-a-modern-lens-of-data-in-context-learning-and-te.md): better test-time scaling, but hurts in-context learning (induction heads).
   * Cheap logits: [sparse logit sampling](2503.16870-sparse-logit-sampling-accelerating-knowledge-distillation-in-llms.md) (unbiased, <10% overhead vs CE), [Efficient Knowledge Distillation for LLMs](2608.03796-efficient-knowledge-distillation-for-llms-offline-top-k-logits-and-a-f.md) (offline top-K +
     fused chunked KL).
   * [Low-Rank Clone](2505.12781-a-token-is-worth-over-1-000-tokens-efficient-knowledge-distillation-th.md): soft-prune teacher weights + activation cloning → 3–7B students matching
     trillion-token models with **20B tokens**.
5. **Reasoning distillation data.** [Small models struggle to learn from strong reasoners](2502.12143-small-models-struggle-to-learn-from-strong-reasoners.md) (ACL'25): mix
   long and short CoT for small students. See also [Unveiling the Key Factors for Distilling Chain-of-Thought Reasoning](2502.18001-unveiling-the-key-factors-for-distilling-chain-of-thought-reasoning.md) and [Scale or Reason?](2509.22193-scale-or-reason-a-compute-equivalent-analysis-of-reasoning-distillatio.md) (compute-equivalent
   analysis).

**Recommendation.**
* *Model builders.* Post-train small models with **SFT cold start → OPD from the strongest same-tokenizer teacher**
  (cross-tokenizer: see [`tokenizer-and-vocab-transfer`](../../model-conversion/tokenizer-and-vocab-transfer/README.md)).
  Budget tens to hundreds of H100-h per 8B student. For several domains, train specialists and combine them with MOPD
  instead of mixing RL.
* *Runtime / infra.* OPD needs a fast **student rollout engine plus teacher log-prob scoring** on the same tokens (a
  prefill-only teacher pass). Batch the teacher scoring as prefix-cached prefill. Offline OPD (Lightning) removes the
  live teacher entirely.
